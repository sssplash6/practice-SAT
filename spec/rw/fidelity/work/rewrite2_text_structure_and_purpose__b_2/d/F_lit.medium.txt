The following text is from Ellen Glasgow’s 1925 novel Barren Ground. The novel is set in Pedlar’s Mill, an impoverished farming community in rural Virginia.

A girl in an orange-coloured shawl stood at the window of Pedlar’s store and looked, through the falling snow, at the deserted road. Though she watched there without moving, her attitude, in its stillness, gave an impression of <u>arrested flight, as if she were running toward life</u>.

<u>Bare, starved, desolate, the country closed in about her.</u> The last train of the day had gone by without stopping, and the station of Pedlar’s Mill was as lonely as the abandoned fields by the track.
