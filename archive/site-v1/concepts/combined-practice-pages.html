Combined Practice Pages

Combined practice, five counters. All interactive — the quantities are the idea, and one tap is one question, and holding a jar or a lever pours faster the longer you hold — the number is live, so you let go when it reads what you want. The sand pours five a tap. One orange live element on each: the scale needle, the dealt pip, the readout, the balance, the pouring stream.
