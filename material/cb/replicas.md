## What the published terms cover

- The [copyright instructions](https://privacy.collegeboard.org/copyright-trademark/request-instructions) say: “Reproduction of any official SAT test materials is not allowed in test prep and other commercial settings.” They also require express written permission to modify or reuse College Board content.
- The [trademark guidelines](https://privacy.collegeboard.org/copyright-trademark/guidelines) say College Board “does not grant permission for its copyrighted content, including practice test questions, to be used in conjunction with generative AI.” They also prohibit training an AI system on College Board content.
- The [copyright instructions](https://privacy.collegeboard.org/copyright-trademark/request-instructions) allow ordinary links to College Board pages. They do not grant rights to copy the linked content.

## Open question for the partner agreement

The public terms address official material and its reproduction. They do not expressly settle whether independently written questions with original wording, values, and explanations may mirror an official item's abstract structure. Obtain a written answer from the College Board partner contact for this exact scenario, including acceptable similarity, examples, and any required review. Until then, keep using original authored content and do not import official stems, answer choices, explanations, screenshots, or variants into the product or AI workflows.
