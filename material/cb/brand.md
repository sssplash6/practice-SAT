## Public rules and product guidance

- **Name:** [College Board's trademark list](https://privacy.collegeboard.org/copyright-trademark/college-board) identifies SAT® as a registered mark and Bluebook™ as a trademark. [The guidelines](https://privacy.collegeboard.org/copyright-trademark/guidelines) allow descriptive titles that clearly identify an independent source (for example, “Smith's App for the SAT® Exam”). They disallow using a mark as part of a company, product, service, or domain name or making the mark more prominent than the product name. Obtain written approval for any proposed branded product title under the partnership before shipping it.
- **Copy:** Use SAT® and Bluebook™ as adjectives with a noun, apply the right symbol, and give the ownership/affiliation disclaimer on each page displaying a mark, as the [guidelines](https://privacy.collegeboard.org/copyright-trademark/guidelines) specify. Refer to the SAT® exam or Bluebook™ testing application accurately. Do not claim College Board endorsement, certification, or a specific partnership scope beyond the written agreement.
- **Screenshots and logos:** The [guidelines](https://privacy.collegeboard.org/copyright-trademark/guidelines) require an express written logo license. The [copyright instructions](https://privacy.collegeboard.org/copyright-trademark/request-instructions) require written permission to reproduce or modify protected materials. Treat screenshots of Bluebook, official questions, and College Board artwork as needing explicit written scope before product or marketing use. Plain text links to official pages are allowed.
- **Advertising:** The [guidelines](https://privacy.collegeboard.org/copyright-trademark/guidelines) restrict use of College Board marks in search and social advertising; have partner contacts approve any exception in writing.
- **Partnership scope:** The user reports an official partnership and email confirmation. This task records the phone permission specified in the helper file; the written agreement was not available for review here. Check the agreement for any negotiated exceptions before changing these defaults.
