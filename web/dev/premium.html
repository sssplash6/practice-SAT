Each specimen opens on the proposal. Switch it to Current to see the app as it ships, replay a motion with the arrow, and Tab through a stage to see focus. IDs such as C4 are how the suggestion list points here.

A. Buttons

The primary gets depth from inside (sheen, lit edge, a dark inset, a ring one step darker, a small amber-tinted drop). Hover is a fill step, never a lift. Press goes down in 100ms and comes back on a soft spring.

B. Depth

An alpha ring instead of a grey border, and warm layered shadows. One raised hero per page; everything else stays flat.

C. Highlights and states

Orange stays the selection colour: picked, current, yours, new.

D. Motion

MOTION.md tokens and springs. Motion explains a change and never holds input; keyboard paths and Reduce motion skip movement (short fades stay).

E. Calm layout

Premium means not clustered: one control row, plain groups, nothing drawn for empty data.

F. Icons

From ICONS.md: Phosphor regular at a held 1.4px stroke, the filled twin for the current item, drawn marks instead of Unicode stand-ins, and one spot drawing from the logo's corners.
